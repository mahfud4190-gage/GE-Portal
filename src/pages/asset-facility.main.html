P39 Domain Guardrails

  • Agreement / Price Schedule remains a commercial domain and is not used as the Asset source of truth.
  • Existing Lounge records are referenced where available; P39 does not create duplicate Lounge masters.
  • Non-Lounge Service Facilities are stored separately from Assets and are selected by reference.
  • Individual tracking requires Asset Tag; quantity tracking does not force individual serial IDs.
  • Retire preserves the Asset record and does not hard-delete operational history.
  • Readiness / Capability formulas are not changed by P39.
  • Evidence is reference-only in P39 because no new Firebase Storage dependency is introduced.
